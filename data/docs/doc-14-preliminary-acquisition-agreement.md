# PRELIMINARY ACQUISITION AGREEMENT (DRAFT v2)

**Between:** Charon Colonial (the **"Seller"**)
**And:** the counterparty designated **"Ferryman"** (the **"Buyer"**). Full legal identity held by the Chair's office only.
**Date of draft:** 20 March 2391
**Status:** Heads of terms agreed in principle. Not yet executed.
**Classification:** DIRECTORATE. Privileged and confidential.

---

## 1. Definitions

- **"Asset"** means the artificial object located beneath the ice of Asphodel, Kepler-1649 system, at survey grid AS-14, together with any components, emissions and data derived from it.
- **"Asset Records"** means all recordings, decodings, logs and medical observations relating to the Asset.
- **"Recovery Operation"** means the Seller's crewed operation to secure the Asset, scheduled for March 2391.

## 2. Exclusivity

2.1 From the date of this draft, the Seller grants the Buyer **exclusive negotiation rights** over the Asset for 180 days.

2.2 The Seller shall not disclose the existence of the Asset to any other charter holder, government or media organisation.

## 3. Consideration

| Delivery condition | Price |
|---|---|
| Asset Records only | 0.2 trillion credits |
| Partial Asset (components) | 1.6 trillion credits |
| **Intact Asset, delivered to Buyer's vessel** | **3.6 trillion credits** |

3.1 A non-refundable deposit of **40 billion credits** is payable on execution.

3.2 The Seller's internal valuation (4.2 trillion credits) is acknowledged by the Seller as an upper bound and is **not** disclosed to the Buyer.

## 4. Seller's warranties

The Seller warrants that:

4.1 It holds the colonial charter for Asphodel and has the right to sell the Asset.

4.2 No personnel involved in the Recovery Operation have rights or claims over the Asset, under Section 14(a) of the Seller's personnel contract.

4.3 All Asset Records will be delivered **without copies retained** by the Seller's personnel.

4.4 Public statements about Asphodel will continue to describe a **mineral survey and colony project only**.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Target delivery date: **30 June 2391**, at a location to be agreed outside the Kepler-1649 system.

5.2 If the Recovery Operation fails, the Buyer may, at its option, fund a **second operation** using colony construction traffic as cover.

## 6. Risk

6.1 The Buyer acknowledges that the Asset's behaviour under interaction is unknown.

6.2 The Seller bears **all risk to its personnel** before delivery. The Buyer accepts no liability for loss of life during the Recovery Operation.

## 7. Timing note (Chair's office, not for circulation to Buyer)

> Ferryman first approached this office on **3 March 2391**, six days before the *Meridian Drift* reported the signal. Their source of information is unknown. The Directorate should assume the Buyer knew about the Asset at least as early as we did.

*Draft prepared by Legal, Office of the Chair. Not valid until signed by both parties.*
