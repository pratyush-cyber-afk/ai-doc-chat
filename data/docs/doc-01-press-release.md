# Charon Survey Discovers Mineral-Rich Moon, New Colony Planned

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE | Charon Colonial Corporate Communications | 14 March 2391**

*"Carrying Humanity Further"*

Charon Colonial is proud to announce a landmark discovery at the outer edge of charted space. The survey vessel **CCV *Meridian Drift***, operated by our Deep-Space Survey division, has completed its initial assessment of **Asphodel**, an icy moon orbiting the gas giant Kepler-1649d in the **Kepler-1649 system**.

The *Meridian Drift* arrived in orbit on **2 March 2391**. Within days, its crew confirmed one of the richest mineral profiles ever recorded by a Charon survey, including substantial deposits of rare-earth metals and a deep subsurface ocean suitable for water extraction.

## A new home on the frontier

Based on these results, Charon Colonial has approved preliminary planning for **Asphodel Station**, a new settlement designed to house an initial population of **12,000 colonists**. The first colonist landing window is scheduled for **Q2 2393**, pending final habitat certification.

Asphodel Station will feature:

- Pressurised habitat domes with full atmosphere processing
- A dedicated medical centre staffed around the clock
- Family housing, schools and recreation facilities
- Guaranteed employment through Charon's Extraction and Colonial Administration divisions

"Asphodel represents everything Charon Colonial stands for," said **Director Cassius Wren**, head of Deep-Space Survey. "Our crews go first, so that families can follow safely. The *Meridian Drift* team has done outstanding work, and we are proud of every one of them."

## Safety first, always

Every Charon colony is built to the company's **Frontier Safety Standard**. Survey teams are conducting routine follow-up scans of the moon's ice shelf, and minor sensor calibration events reported during the survey are being reviewed as standard procedure. Charon Colonial does not anticipate any impact on the colony timeline.

## Investor information

The Asphodel project is expected to strengthen Charon Colonial's long-term extraction portfolio across the Kepler sector. Further details will be shared at the next shareholder briefing.

## About Charon Colonial

Charon Colonial is the largest charter holder in known space, operating in more than 140 star systems through its Terraforming, Deep-Space Survey, Extraction and Colonial Administration divisions. For over a century, Charon has helped humanity build new lives among the stars.
