# BOARD MEMORANDUM: DIRECTORATE EYES ONLY

**To:** Chairwoman Odile Marchetti and Members of the Directorate
**From:** Director Cassius Wren, Deep-Space Survey
**Date:** 12 March 2391
**Subject:** Strategic Value of the Asphodel Source
**Distribution:** 7 copies. Not to be transmitted below Directorate level.

---

## 1. Purpose

To brief the Directorate on the source detected beneath Asphodel (ref. TA-1649-07), to present a preliminary valuation, and to request authority for recovery operations.

## 2. Background

The detection did not come as a surprise to this office. In **2384**, the uncrewed probe *Acheron-3* recorded a faint, intermittent emission from the Kepler-1649 system during a long-range pass. The finding was classified under **Project Styx** and the system was quietly prioritised for survey. The *Meridian Drift* was not informed. Its mission profile was presented to the crew as a routine mineral assessment.

Dr. Okafor's preliminary decoding confirms what Project Styx suspected: the source is artificial, structured, and appears to be **deliberately signalling its own location**.

## 3. Valuation

Our analysts consider the source to be non-human technology, potentially intact. Even partial recovery would represent the most valuable asset in Charon Colonial's history.

| Scenario | Estimated value |
|---|---|
| Data only (signal fully decoded) | 0.3 trillion credits |
| Partial physical recovery | 1.9 trillion credits |
| Intact recovery of source | **4.2 trillion credits** |

For context, the projected 40-year return of the entire Asphodel Station colony is 0.06 trillion credits. The colony is now of value principally as **cover for an extended operational presence** on the moon.

## 4. Risk to personnel

The source's behaviour under interaction is unknown. Recovery will require a crewed landing at grid AS-14 and descent to the cavity.

The estimated cost of total loss of the *Meridian Drift* crew (seven personnel), including contractual death benefits, legal exposure and replacement, is **38 million credits**. Under Section 14(c) of the personnel contract, benefits are void if loss results from a breach of company directives, which would reduce the figure substantially.

This office considers the risk acceptable.

## 5. Information control

The following measures are already in place:

- Communications routing through Lethe-9 under Policy CCP-7.4
- Public messaging framing the detection as "minor sensor calibration events" (approved by the Chair, 11 March)
- Corporate Liaison E. Vasquez holding reporting authority aboard the vessel

Captain Valen is assessed as **unreliable** in the event of a recovery order. He is likely to place crew safety ahead of the objective.

## 6. Commercial interest

Discreet preliminary contact has been made with a third party, referred to in this memo only as **"Ferryman"**, who has expressed interest in exclusive acquisition rights. Terms will follow under separate cover.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Authorise recovery of the Asphodel source as the Directorate's **highest operational priority**.
2. Issue a directive placing landing command authority with the Corporate Liaison, superseding the captain where necessary.
3. Proceed with the Asphodel Station colony announcement as planned to maintain public cover.
4. Continue negotiations with Ferryman before the crew lands.

*C. Wren*
*Director, Deep-Space Survey*
