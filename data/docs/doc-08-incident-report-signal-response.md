# Incident Report IR-1649-02: Signal Response Event

**Vessel:** CCV *Meridian Drift*
**Location:** Orbit of Asphodel, Kepler-1649 system
**Incident date/time:** 15 March 2391, 02:47 ship time
**Reported by:** Captain Idris Valen
**Technical input:** Kofi Mensah (Chief Engineer), Dr. Mara Okafor (Science Officer)
**Related report:** TA-1649-07
**Classification:** Survey: Restricted
**Status:** OPEN

---

## 1. Summary

At 02:47 ship time on 15 March 2391, Communications Specialist **Teo Lindqvist** transmitted an unauthorised signal from the ship's primary comms array toward the source at grid **AS-14**. The transmission repeated the source's own prime-number primer on **1420.4 MHz**.

This directly contravened Recommendation 1 of TA-1649-07 (**no transmissions toward the source**). Within one cycle, the source's behaviour changed.

## 2. Sequence of events

| Time (ship) | Event |
|---|---|
| 02:31 | Lindqvist logs into comms array using his own credentials during night watch |
| 02:47 | Transmission sent: primes 2 to 23, matched to the 47.3 s cycle |
| 02:48 | Source misses its next scheduled cycle: **first gap in 1,826+ cycles** |
| 02:50 | Source resumes. Repeat interval now **varies**; no longer fixed at 47.3 s |
| 03:12 | Okafor (woken by automated alert) confirms the dense layer has changed |
| 03:40 | Mensah isolates the comms array at the captain's order |
| 06:00 | Lindqvist relieved of comms duties, confined to quarters pending review |

## 3. Observed changes in the source

1. **Timing.** The interval now shifts between 41 and 53 seconds. Okafor reports the shifts match the rhythm of human speech.
2. **Content.** Fragments of the dense layer, when converted to audio, now contain **recognisable sounds from the ship's own internal channels**: alert tones, the galley timer, and at least one phrase in Lindqvist's voice.
3. **Mirroring.** From 14:00 on 15 March, the source began repeating short pieces of crew communications within minutes of them being sent, including traffic sent only on internal channels that should not be detectable outside the hull.

Mensah has checked for signal leakage from the hull three times and found none. In his words: *"It's not hearing us through the walls. I don't know how it's hearing us."*

## 4. Root cause

- Lindqvist states he acted alone because HQ had not responded to the 10 March request for guidance, and he believed "somebody had to say hello."
- The comms array had no second-person lock for outbound transmissions toward surface coordinates. This is a **procedural gap**.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Two-person authorisation now required for all outbound transmissions (owner: Mensah, **done**).
- Comms array keys reissued; Lindqvist's credentials revoked (owner: Mensah, **done**).
- Crew instructed not to discuss the source on internal channels until the mirroring is understood (owner: Captain, **done**).
- Request to HQ for immediate guidance and permission to withdraw to a higher orbit (owner: Captain, **sent via Lethe-9, no response**).

## 6. Liaison comment

> Under Handbook Section 14(c), Specialist Lindqvist's action is a breach of contract. A disciplinary record has been opened. Withdrawal to a higher orbit is **not approved** pending Directorate review.
> E. Vasquez, Corporate Liaison
