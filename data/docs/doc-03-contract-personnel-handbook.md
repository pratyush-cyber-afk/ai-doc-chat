# Contract Personnel Handbook (Excerpt)

**Charon Colonial | Office of Colonial Administration**
**Revision 31, effective 1 July 2390**
**Classification:** Internal: Personnel

*This excerpt covers Sections 9, 12 and 14. The full handbook is available from your Corporate Liaison or Colonial Administration office.*

---

## Section 9: Chain of command aboard Charon vessels

**9(a)** The vessel's captain holds command authority over navigation, crew safety and day-to-day operations.

**9(b)** The Corporate Liaison assigned to a vessel represents the interests of Charon Colonial and holds **reporting authority** over all communications with company headquarters.

**9(c)** Where a **Directorate-level directive** is in force, command authority for the activities named in that directive passes to the person designated in the directive. This may include the Corporate Liaison. The captain retains authority over all other matters.

**9(d)** Personnel must comply with lawful orders from whichever officer holds authority under 9(a)–9(c). Disputes are to be logged and raised after the operation, not during it.

---

## Section 12: Pay, bonuses and return passage

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Base pay | Paid monthly into a Charon Colonial credit account |
| Hazard allowance | Payable only for activities classified as hazardous **in advance** by Colonial Administration |
| Completion bonus | Paid on successful completion of the full contract term |
| Return passage | Allocated by **priority schedule**; priority is set by service record |
| Transit and housing costs | Recovered by monthly wage deduction |

**12(a)** Activities not classified as hazardous in advance do not attract hazard allowance, even if they are later found to have been hazardous.

**12(b)** Return passage priority may be reduced for disciplinary reasons. Personnel with reduced priority are transported when space allows.

---

## Section 14: Conduct, confidentiality and breach of contract

**14(a)** All survey data, sensor readings, coordinates and mission findings are the property of Charon Colonial from the moment they are recorded.

**14(b)** Personnel must not disclose company information to any third party, including family members, without written approval from Colonial Administration.

**14(c)** The following constitute a **breach of contract**:

- bypassing approved communications routing, including use of unregistered transmitters or personal relay accounts
- refusing or delaying a lawful order issued under Section 9
- disclosing company information in breach of 14(b)
- taking any action toward an external party, object or signal without authorisation from the officer holding authority under Section 9

**14(d)** A breach of contract may result in:

- loss of completion bonus
- forfeiture of return passage priority
- referral to Charon Security
- **voiding of all contractual death and injury benefits** where the death or injury arises from, or during, the breach

**14(e)** By signing a Charon Colonial contract, personnel confirm that they have read and understood this section.
