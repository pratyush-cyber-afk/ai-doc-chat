# Science Officer's Log: Dr. Mara Okafor

*CCV Meridian Drift. Personal log, voice-to-text transcription. Entries 2391-03-09 to 2391-03-14.*

---

**9 March 2391, 06:40**

Couldn't sleep, so I was on the radar pass when it came in. I thought it was noise at first. Then it repeated. Then it repeated again, exactly 47.3 seconds later, and kept doing it.

Idris asked me if I was sure. I told him I've spent eleven years calibrating these arrays and I've never seen noise keep time like a metronome. Kofi checked the hardware twice and said, in his words, "The equipment is boring and fine. The moon is the problem."

---

**10 March 2391, 22:15**

Filed TA-1649-07. The prime sequence is unmistakable: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, all the way to 23. Nothing in nature counts in primes. Someone, or something, wanted to be noticed.

What bothers me is the dense layer underneath. It's not random. The entropy is too low for noise and too high for a simple repeat. It's information.

Vasquez blocked my recommendation to brief the whole crew. Policy CCP-7.4, she said, as if that explains anything. Idris argued with her for twenty minutes behind a closed door. He lost.

---

**12 March 2391, 03:05**

I think I've cracked the structure of the dense layer.

It's written in **base 7**. Once you read it that way, the first block is a counting primer: one mark, two marks, three, the way you'd teach a child. Then simple arithmetic. Then something that looks very much like a **map of this star system**, with Kepler-1649 at the centre and Asphodel highlighted.

Highlighted. As in: *here*.

I've started calling it **"the Choir"** in my notes, because when you render the dense layer as audio it sounds like many voices holding one note. Unscientific, I know. I'm keeping it out of the official reports.

---

**13 March 2391, 19:50**

Teo came to the lab tonight. He's been reading over my shoulder for days. He asked what would happen if we sent the primer back, with the same primes, the same frequency, just to say *we heard you*.

I told him exactly what TA-1649-07 says: **no transmissions toward the source** until HQ responds. We don't know what it does when it hears an answer. We don't even know if "answer" is the right word.

He said HQ isn't going to respond, not really, and that someone has to make the first move. I don't think he was joking.

I'm going to talk to Idris about locking down the comms array.

---

**14 March 2391, 11:30**

The company press release came through the relay this morning. Apparently we discovered a "mineral-rich moon" and observed "minor sensor calibration events."

Calibration events.

There's a structured signal 2.1 kilometres under the ice, using the hydrogen line, counting in primes, drawing maps, and the official word is *calibration*. Twelve thousand colonists are going to be told this place is safe.

Kofi read it over my shoulder and laughed for a full minute. Then he stopped laughing.

I asked Vasquez who approved the wording. She said it came "from above Director Wren." I didn't know there *was* an above Director Wren, apart from the board.

Personal note, for whoever reads this later: I don't think they're surprised by any of this. I think they were expecting something.

*End of entries.*
