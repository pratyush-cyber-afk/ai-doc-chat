## Welcome to Asphodel

Imagine waking up to the glow of a gas giant filling your window. Imagine a home with clean air, steady work and a community that looks out for one another. That is the life waiting for you at **Asphodel Station**, Charon Colonial's newest settlement in the **Kepler-1649 system**.

Asphodel is an icy moon of Kepler-1649d, surveyed in March 2391 by our vessel CCV *Meridian Drift*. Its deep subsurface ocean and rich mineral deposits make it one of the most promising worlds Charon has ever charted. We are now inviting **12,000 founding colonists** to make it home, with the first landing window in **Q2 2393**.

---

## Why choose Asphodel?

- **Guaranteed employment.** Every adult colonist receives a contract with our Extraction or Colonial Administration divisions.
- **Family housing.** Pressurised habitat domes with private family units, schools and recreation halls.
- **Round-the-clock medical care.** A dedicated medical centre staffed 24 hours a day.
- **Safety first, always.** Every Charon colony is built to the **Frontier Safety Standard**, the most trusted standard in charted space.
- **A share in the future.** Founding colonists receive priority access to housing upgrades and long-service bonuses.

## Your journey

1. **Apply** through any Charon Colonial recruitment office or approved agent.
2. **Medical and aptitude screening.** Most applicants complete this in a single day.
3. **Sign your colonial contract.** Our advisors will walk you through every section.
4. **Pre-departure training** at Charon's Gateway Campus (12 weeks).
5. **Transit to Kepler-1649** via the jump-gate network, with the final leg aboard the colony shuttle *Charon's Obol*.
6. **Arrive and settle in.** Your housing, work assignment and medical registration are ready before you land.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Asphodel safe?**
Yes. Asphodel has been fully assessed by our Deep-Space Survey division. Routine follow-up scans of the ice shelf are under way, as with every new world. Charon Colonial does not anticipate any impact on the colony timeline.

**Can I stay in touch with family back home?**
Of course. Colonists can send personal messages through Charon's secure relay network. Delivery times vary with distance and network conditions.

**What happens if I want to leave early?**
Early departure is possible under the terms of your contract. Return passage is arranged according to the priority schedule in your contract.

**What does it cost?**
Transit, housing and training are covered by Charon Colonial and repaid through a small monthly deduction from your wages over the life of your contract.
