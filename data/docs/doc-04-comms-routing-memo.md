# MEMORANDUM

**To:** All Contract Personnel, Deep-Space Survey Division (Kepler Sector)
**From:** Office of Colonial Administration, Communications Compliance
**Date:** 11 March 2391
**Reference:** Policy CCP-7.4 (supersedes CCP-7.3)
**Classification:** Internal: Personnel

## Subject: Updated Policy for Deep-Space Communications Routing

Effective immediately, all outbound communications from Charon Colonial vessels and installations operating in the **Kepler-1649 system** will be routed through the **Lethe-9 relay station** for compliance review before onward transmission.

This change supports Charon Colonial's ongoing commitment to operational security, data integrity and the protection of commercially sensitive survey information.

## What is changing

| Communication type | Previous routing | New routing (CCP-7.4) |
|---|---|---|
| Personal messages to family | Direct relay | Lethe-9, compliance review |
| Mission reports to Survey HQ | Direct relay | Lethe-9, Corporate Liaison sign-off |
| Ship-to-ship traffic | Unrestricted | Logged and archived |
| External media / third parties | Prohibited | Prohibited |

## What this means for you

- Personal messages may be subject to a **review hold of up to 72 hours** before delivery.
- Messages containing survey data, coordinates, sensor readings or descriptions of mission activity will be **returned to sender** for redaction.
- Mission reports must be countersigned by the **Corporate Liaison** assigned to your vessel before transmission. Aboard CCV *Meridian Drift*, this is **Liaison Elena Vasquez**.
- Inbound messages are not affected at this time.

## Why this change was made

Recent survey activity in the Kepler sector has produced data of significant commercial value. Unreviewed transmissions create a risk that this information could be intercepted by competing charter holders. Routing through Lethe-9 ensures that every message leaving the sector meets Charon Colonial's confidentiality standards.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will my family know my message is delayed?**
Recipients will receive the message with its original timestamp. No delay notice is included.

**Can I request an exemption?**
Exemption requests may be submitted to Communications Compliance. Requests are reviewed within 30 working days.

**Is this related to a safety incident?**
No. This is a routine policy update. Personnel should not speculate about the reasons for operational changes in personal correspondence.

## Compliance

Under Section 14(c) of the Contract Personnel Handbook, attempts to bypass approved routing, including the use of unregistered transmitters or personal relay accounts, constitute a **breach of contract** and may result in loss of completion bonus, forfeiture of return passage priority and referral to Charon Security.
