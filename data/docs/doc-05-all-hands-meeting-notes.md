# Quarterly All-Hands Meeting Notes: Q1 2391

**Division:** Deep-Space Survey, Kepler Sector
**Date:** 18 March 2391
**Format:** Recorded broadcast, relayed via Lethe-9 (delayed delivery to vessels)
**Chair:** Director Cassius Wren
**Notes taken by:** Colonial Administration, Communications Compliance
**Classification:** Internal: Personnel

---

## 1. Opening remarks (Director Wren)

- Thanked all survey crews for "a record quarter".
- Highlighted the *Meridian Drift* team's work at Asphodel as "the discovery of the decade" for the division.
- Confirmed the Asphodel Station colony is on schedule: **12,000 colonists**, first landing **Q2 2393**. Recruitment opened on 21 March.

## 2. Operational updates

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Asphodel mineral assessment | Complete |
| Ice shelf follow-up scans | Ongoing, "routine" |
| Colony habitat certification | In progress |
| Communications policy CCP-7.4 | In force since 11 March |
| *Charon's Obol* shuttle refit | Ahead of schedule |

## 3. Communications policy (Communications Compliance)

- Reminder that all outbound traffic from Kepler-1649 now passes through the **Lethe-9 relay** with a review hold of up to **72 hours**.
- 41 personal messages from the sector were returned to sender for redaction in the first week. Staff were reminded not to describe "mission activity" in personal messages.
- Exemption requests: 6 received, 0 approved so far.

## 4. Questions from crews

Questions were submitted in advance through the Corporate Liaison channel.

**Q (CCV *Meridian Drift*, name withheld):** Are the "sensor calibration events" mentioned in the press release a safety issue?
**A (Director Wren):** No. Survey teams are reviewing the readings as standard procedure. Speculation does not help anyone, and I would ask crews to leave analysis to the people whose job it is.

**Q (CCV *Meridian Drift*, name withheld):** Why do mission reports now need Liaison sign-off instead of the captain's?
**A (Communications Compliance):** This brings the Kepler sector in line with Section 9(b) of the Contract Personnel Handbook. The captain's authority is otherwise unchanged.

**Q (Extraction crew, Kepler-1649d orbital platform):** Will hazard allowance apply to ice shelf work?
**A (Colonial Administration):** Not at this time. Ice shelf scanning has not been classified as hazardous.

**Q (CCV *Meridian Drift*, name withheld):** Has HQ received our request for guidance on the readings from 10 March?
**A:** *Not answered during the session. Referred to Director Wren's office.*

## 5. Closing

Director Wren closed by reminding staff that "the colony depends on the confidence of the people who will live there" and asked everyone to "keep our messaging consistent."
