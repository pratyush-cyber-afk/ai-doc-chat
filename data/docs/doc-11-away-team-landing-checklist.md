## Phase 2: Landing and surface

- [ ] **6.** Land at AS-14 marker beacon. Confirm ice stability readings
- [ ] **7.** Deploy drilling rig over the source coordinates
- [ ] **8.** Begin descent bore toward cavity (~2.1 km). Estimated time: 9 hours
- [ ] **9.** Passive listening only. **No transmissions toward the source** (TA-1649-07, Rec. 1)

## Phase 3: Cavity entry

- [ ] **10.** Camera drone enters the cavity first (approx. 340 m diameter)
- [ ] **11.** Two-person team descends on tether: Park + Okafor
- [ ] **12.** Retrieve samples and imaging of the source
- [ ] **13.** *[ADDED BY COLONIAL ADMINISTRATION]* Prepare source for **physical extraction** if feasible. Liaison decision is final.

## Abort criteria

Abort and return to the shuttle if any of the following occur:

- Ice instability above threshold
- Suit failure or medical emergency
- Loss of contact with *Meridian Drift* for more than 10 minutes
- ~~Captain's order~~ *(struck by Colonial Administration, see 4a)*
