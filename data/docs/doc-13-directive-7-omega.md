# DIRECTIVE 7-OMEGA: RECOVERY PRIORITY

**Issued by:** The Directorate of Charon Colonial
**Authorised by:** Chairwoman Odile Marchetti
**Prepared by:** Director Cassius Wren, Deep-Space Survey
**Date of issue:** 22 March 2391
**Distribution:** Directorate members; Corporate Liaison E. Vasquez (CCV *Meridian Drift*), Sections 3 and 4 only
**Classification:** DIRECTORATE. Existence of this directive is not to be disclosed below Directorate level.

---

## 1. Authority

This directive is issued under the Directorate's charter powers and Section 9(c) of the Contract Personnel Handbook. It follows the board's acceptance of the recommendations in the memo *Strategic Value of the Asphodel Source* (12 March 2391).

## 2. Objective

Recovery of the Asphodel source at grid AS-14 is designated the **highest operational priority** of Charon Colonial. All other objectives in the Kepler-1649 system, including crew welfare, colony preparation and scientific study, are **secondary** to recovery.

## 3. Command arrangements

1. On launch of any landing operation, command authority for that operation passes to **Corporate Liaison Elena Vasquez**.
2. The captain's abort authority is **suspended** for the duration of the operation.
3. The Liaison shall not accept abort requests on medical grounds unless the affected person is physically unable to continue.
4. Any refusal of orders is to be logged as a breach under Section 14(c), with consequences under 14(d) applied in full.

## 4. Operational orders to the Liaison

- Land at AS-14 no later than **28 March 2391**.
- Prepare the source for **physical extraction**. Imaging and samples alone do not satisfy this directive.
- Maintain the communications hold through Lethe-9. No outbound crew messages are to be released until the operation is complete.
- Requests for withdrawal, specialist support or medical evacuation are to be **acknowledged but not actioned**.
- Specialist Lindqvist is to remain confined. His interaction with the source is of research interest and his quarters' audio is to be recorded.

## 5. Acceptable loss

The Directorate has reviewed the risk assessment in the 12 March memo. Loss of the away team, or of the full crew, is **accepted** if the source is recovered or its position secured for a follow-up operation.

In the event of crew loss:

- Deaths are to be classified as resulting from breach of directive where the record supports it, so that benefits under 14(d) are void.
- Public statements will describe an **"ice shelf collapse during routine survey"**.
- The Asphodel Station colony timeline is **not** to be changed. Recruitment continues.

## 6. Handover

The recovered source, all signal recordings, Dr. Okafor's notes and the medical log of Dr. Reyes are to be sealed for transfer to the acquiring party named in the draft agreement of 20 March. No copies are to be retained aboard the *Meridian Drift*.

## 7. Review

This directive remains in force until revoked by the Chair. Reports under this directive go to Director Wren only.

*By order of the Directorate.*
*O. Marchetti, Chair*
